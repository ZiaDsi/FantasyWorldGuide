What is Fantasy?

Fantasy is a genre of fiction that explores imaginary worlds, magic, and supernatural elements.

Explore Fantasy Subgenres

Choose a subgenre to explore its characteristics and discover what makes it unique.

Epic Fantasy

Epic Fantasy focuses on expansive imaginary worlds, large-scale conflicts, and extraordinary adventures.

Common Elements

  • Detailed worldbuilding
  • Magic and mythical creatures
  • Kingdoms, wars, and political conflicts

Dark Fantasy

Dark Fantasy combines fantasy elements with darker themes, unsettling atmospheres, and moral complexity.

Common Elements

  • Horror-inspired atmosphere
  • Morally complex characters
  • Dangerous supernatural forces

Urban Fantasy

Urban Fantasy introduces magic and supernatural elements into modern or recognizable urban environments.

Common Elements

  • Modern cities
  • Hidden magical communities
  • Supernatural mysteries
